3. What We Collect

3.1Information you give us. Name, business name, role, email address, telephone number, company registration number, billing address and payment reference when you open an Account, buy from the shop or make an enquiry.
3.2Information about your use of the Platform. Login records, the Blueprints you run, the number of Runs and Counsel Credits consumed, booking records, support messages and correspondence with us.
3.3Consultation information. Where you book a Counsel Session or engage TSL on a scoped matter, the information you give us about the matter, our notes and the advice given. TSL is the responsible party for this information because it determines how the information is processed in order to advise you and to meet its own record keeping obligations.
3.4Information collected automatically. IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed and time spent, and cookie identifiers. Clause 12 explains cookies.
3.5Information from third parties. Payment confirmation and fraud screening results from our payment provider, information from a referral partner where you reach us through one, and publicly available company records from the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission where we verify a business.
3.6What we do not collect. We do not collect card numbers. Card details are entered on the payment provider's systems and are never passed to us. We do not knowingly collect information about children through the website or the Platform.

4. Why We Process It, and on What Basis

4.1POPIA requires a lawful ground for each purpose. Ours are set out below.
4.2To provide the services you have asked for, including operating your Account, generating documents, scheduling Counsel Sessions and giving advice. Ground: necessary for the performance of a contract with you, under section 11(1)(b).
4.3To take payment and to keep accounting records. Grounds: performance of a contract under section 11(1)(b), and compliance with a legal obligation under section 11(1)(c), including the Tax Administration Act 28 of 2011 and the Companies Act 71 of 2008.
4.4To run a conflict check before a Counsel Session or an engagement. Ground: legitimate interests under section 11(1)(f), being our interest and yours in not acting where a conflict exists.
4.5To provide support, respond to enquiries and handle complaints. Ground: performance of a contract, or legitimate interests where you are not yet a customer.
4.6To secure the Platform, prevent fraud and investigate misuse. Ground: legitimate interests under section 11(1)(f).
4.7To understand how the website and Platform are used and to improve them. Ground: consent for analytics cookies under clause 12, and legitimate interests for aggregated statistics that do not identify anyone.
4.8To send you marketing. Ground: consent, except where clause 5.3 applies.
4.9To comply with the law and to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim. Ground: section 11(1)(c) and section 11(1)(d).

8. How Long We Keep It

8.1Account Data: for as long as your Account is open, and for five years after closure, to deal with a query or a claim.
8.2Billing and accounting records: five years from the end of the tax year to which they relate, as required by section 29 of the Tax Administration Act 28 of 2011.
8.3Consultation and matter records: five years from the conclusion of the matter.
8.4Generated Documents and Evidence Packs: while your Account is open, then for thirty days after closure so that you can download them, then deleted in accordance with clause 10 of the Operator and Data Processing Addendum.
8.5Support and enquiry correspondence: two years from the last message.
8.6Marketing records, including consents and objections: for as long as we market to you, and for three years after you object, so that we can show we acted on the objection.
8.7Security and access logs: twelve months.
8.8Website analytics identifiers: thirteen months.
8.9Where a record is relevant to an actual or anticipated claim, we keep it until the claim is resolved and the period for an appeal has passed.
8.10At the end of a retention period we delete the record or de-identify it so that it can no longer be linked to you.

9. How We Protect It

9.1We take appropriate and reasonable technical and organisational measures under section 19 of POPIA. The measures are listed in Annexure B to the Operator and Data Processing Addendum and include encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access control, multi-factor authentication on administrative accounts, access logging, environment separation, encrypted backups with restore testing, and a documented security compromise procedure.
9.2If a security compromise affects your personal information, we notify the Information Regulator and, where section 22 of POPIA requires it, you, as soon as reasonably possible after establishing the scope of the compromise.
9.3No system is completely secure. Keep your login credentials confidential and tell us within twenty four hours if you think they have been compromised.

11. Automated Processing and Artificial Intelligence

11.1The Platform assembles documents automatically from the answers given, and applies Gates that test those answers against published conditions. That is automation applied to a document, not to a person.
11.2TSL does not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that produce a legal effect or affect you to a substantial degree, within the meaning of section 71 of POPIA. A Gate that stops a Run does not decide anything about you. It routes the Run to a Counsel Session with a person.
11.3TSL does not use the information you submit to train machine learning models. Where TSL uses a third party service that involves automated processing of your information, that provider is engaged as a sub-operator under a contract that prohibits use of the information for training or for the provider's own purposes.
11.4TSL uses anonymised and aggregated usage statistics, which cannot identify you, to operate and improve the Platform.

12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

12.1A cookie is a small text file placed on your device when you visit a website. We also use similar technologies such as local storage and pixels. In this clause all of them are called cookies.
12.2We use four categories:
12.2.1Strictly necessary. Session management, authentication, load balancing, protection against cross-site request forgery, and remembering your cookie choices. The website and the Platform do not work without these. They are placed without consent because they are necessary to provide the service you have asked for.
12.2.2Functional. Language and display preferences, and saving your progress part way through a Run so that you do not lose your answers. Placed with your consent.
12.2.3Analytics. Understanding which pages and Blueprints are used, where people leave, and how the Platform performs. Placed with your consent.
12.2.4Marketing. Measuring the response to our campaigns and limiting how often you see the same message. Placed with your consent.
12.3Your choices. On your first visit a banner asks you to accept or reject each category other than strictly necessary cookies. Rejecting is as easy as accepting. You can change your choice at any time through the cookie settings link in the footer of every page.
12.4You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will stop parts of the Platform from working.
12.5Retention. Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies last no longer than thirteen months, after which you are asked again.
12.6Third party cookies. Our analytics provider and our payment provider set cookies on our pages for the purposes described above. They are engaged as sub-operators and may not use the data for their own purposes.
12.7We honour a browser signal that expresses a choice about tracking where the signal is one we are able to recognise.
